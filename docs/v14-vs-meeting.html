5 · What to take back to the team

Conflicts to settle

  1. Reopen skipped stages? Your “activate the disabled parts” vs their R6 “an omitted stage never reopens”.
  2. Analítica stays in the menu, with the funnel per colocación, or is folded into Dashboard.
  3. Groups in the sidebar: keep a recent-clients list, or not.
  4. Mail in the MVP: internal to the ejecutivo only (compatible with R43), or nothing.

Your ideas to add to their roadmap

  • SSO (Google/Microsoft) at launch.
  • Mail on behalf of the broker: needs per-broker domain setup, so it’s its own project.
  • Portal RPA (Playwright + proxy) and lead-search agents in the “Later” phase, after the machine exists. Settle credential custody and each insurer’s portal terms first.
  • One generic agent instead of many isolated ones: an architecture principle for the “Agentes IA” item.
Bottom line: you and the team understood the same product. The core (partial journeys, the colocación as parent, cobranza first, policies feeding cobranza) is fully aligned. The differences are about the menu (Analítica, groups), timing (ForPay, agents, mail) and one rule (reopening skipped stages). Nothing here requires redesigning their document.
